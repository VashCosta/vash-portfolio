It provides comprehensive parsing and evaluation of candidates' core technical competencies, professional strengths, experience depth, and specific job-role suitability scoring.

The system leverages advanced semantic job-matching algorithms to perform in-depth candidate suitability evaluation, generating personalized feedback and identifying critical skill gaps against modern job market standards.
